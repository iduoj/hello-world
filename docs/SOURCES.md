# Sources and verification notes

This file records what was checked for each section, where sources disagree, and what still needs checking against full text.

Research was done with web search in November 2026. Several primary sites were blocked from this environment: ahajournals.org, scai.org, syntaxscore.org, litfl.com and parts of EuroIntervention. Many values were therefore confirmed from abstracts and secondary summaries of those sources. Values marked **[standard teaching]** are widely taught but were not re-confirmed against a source in this pass.

## Anatomy and segment definitions (SYNTAX / modified AHA)
- **RCA:**
  - Proximal (1): ostium to halfway to the acute margin.
  - Mid (2): to the acute margin.
  - Distal (3): acute margin to the PDA origin.
  - PDA (4); right posterolateral (16, 16a–c).
- **LM (5).**
- **LAD:**
  - Proximal (6): to and including the first major septal.
  - Mid (7): to where the LAD forms an angle in RAO, or halfway to the apex.
  - Apical (8).
  - D1 (9/9a), D2 (10/10a).
- **LCx:**
  - Proximal (11): to and including the OM1 origin.
  - Ramus/intermediate (12); OMs (12a/12b).
  - Distal (13): beyond the last OM.
  - L-PL (14); L-PDA (15).
- **Numbering conflict:** SYNTAX has no segment 17. "17 = ramus" comes from the SCCT 18-segment CT model.
- **Ostial lesion:** within 3 mm of the origin.
- **Bifurcation side-branch size:** SYNTAX uses ≥1.5 mm; Bif-ARC uses ≥2.0 mm.
- **Dominance:** right ≈70–85%, left ≈5–10%, codominant the rest; prevalence varies with the definition used.
- **SA nodal artery:** from the RCA in ≈55–68% of hearts. Its origin does not follow dominance.
- **Conus:** has a separate ostium in ≈20–50%.
- Sources: Sianos 2005 (EuroIntervention), syntaxscore.org tutorial, StatPearls NBK537207, Radiopaedia.

## Angiographic projections
- **Naming:** a view is named by where the detector sits.
- **Recognizing the view:** spine on the image right = LAO; image left = RAO. Diaphragm in the field = cranial.
- **Standard view angles and uses** follow EuroIntervention "Tools & techniques: angiographic views", the BJC trainee guide and the USC teaching set.
- **Disagreements:**
  - The best view for the LM ostium: AP vs a shallow oblique with caudal tilt.
  - Exact angles vary between sources and with body habitus.
- **Our 3D model:** built from anatomical principles, with a long axis pointing left, anterior and inferior and a non-diametral septum. It reproduces the classic appearances: RAO caudal LCx/OMs, cranial views opening the LAD and diagonals, the RCA "C" in LAO and "L" in RAO. It is schematic, and real hearts vary in orientation.

## Catheters
- **JL number:** the distance between the primary and secondary curves.
- **Size changes:**
  - Use about 0.5 smaller from the right radial.
  - Use a larger curve in dilated roots.
  - A curve that is too small sits low in the sinus; one that is too big points up ("stands up"). This is mechanical reasoning plus **[standard teaching]**.
- **Tiger and Jacky:** sizes from Terumo product pages. Kane 2022 RCT gave procedure times; JUDGE compared Tiger II with Judkins.
- **Guides:**
  - EBU sizing per the EuroIntervention guide-catheter "Tools & techniques" article.
  - French size ≈ OD × 3.
  - Rotablator burr / guide size per the Boston Scientific table. The 6 Fr maximum is 1.5 mm in one source and 1.75 mm "with friction" in another.
- **Still unverified:** Williams 3DRC, Barbeau and Ikari R details, and the Jacky curve geometry (the drawings are schematic).

## Drugs
- **Verapamil (radial):** 2.5–5 mg IA. In pooled data 5 mg was most effective. Contraindications per StatPearls.
- **Diltiazem:** 5 mg was inferior in SPASM3.
- **Nicardipine:** the RCT dose was 400 µg; there is no standard dose.
- **Radial heparin:** 5000 U or 50 U/kg (SCAI 2014/2020); SPIRIT OF ARTEMIS supports higher doses.
- **IC NTG:** 100–200 µg.
- **No-reflow (SCAI table):**
  - Adenosine 50–200 µg
  - Verapamil 100–250 µg
  - Nicardipine 50–200 µg
  - Nitroprusside 50–200 µg
  - Epinephrine 50–200 µg
  - No consensus maximum cumulative doses were found.
- **Hyperemia:**
  - IV adenosine 140 µg/kg/min.
  - IC adenosine 100 µg RCA / 200 µg LCA (2023 Asia-Pacific consensus); older protocols used lower doses.
  - Papaverine 8–12 mg.
  - Regadenoson 400 µg.
- **ACT targets:** 250–300 s (HemoTec) / 300–350 s (Hemochron) without GPI; 200–250 s with GPI.
- **Bivalirudin:** per the DailyMed label.
- **Protamine:** 1 mg per 100 U, max 50 mg, ≤5 mg/min.
- **ACR contrast reaction card:** epinephrine doses and premedication regimens.
- **Still to verify:**
  - Concern about protamine with a fresh stent **[standard teaching]**.
  - Extra bivalirudin bolus rules.

## Physiology
- **Cut-offs:** FFR ≤0.80 (grey zone 0.75–0.80); iFR, RFR and dPR ≤0.89; resting Pd/Pa ≤0.91.
- **Technique:** iFR wave-free period definition; drift check 0.98–1.02.
- **Trials:**
  - DEFER, FAME 2, DEFINE-FLAIR, iFR-SWEDEHEART, FAME 3 (5-year), FLOWER-MI, FAVOR III.
  - The FAME 1 1-year numbers are **[standard teaching]**.
- **2021 ACC/AHA/SCAI:** FFR/iFR is Class 1 (A) for intermediate stenoses; PCI when FFR >0.80 or iFR >0.89 is Class 3 (no benefit).
- **Simulator:** a lumped Gould-equation model calibrated to typical QCA–FFR relationships. It is a teaching model, not a predictive calculator.

## ECG and echo
- **STEMI thresholds:** 4th UDMI.
  - V4R ≥0.5 mm per UDMI vs ≥1 mm classic; both are shown.
  - Diffuse STD in ≥6 leads per UDMI vs ≥8 leads in older documents.
  - **A Fifth UDMI (2026) has been published; its ECG thresholds were not reviewed here.**
- **Localization:**
  - Engelen 1999 for LAD occlusion site.
  - LITFL for high lateral, inferior, RV, posterior, Wellens, de Winter and aVR.
  - The RCA vs LCx criteria lose accuracy in validation cohorts.
- **Wellens:** type A ≈25% and type B ≈75%; some sources reverse the labels.
- **Echo:**
  - AHA 17-segment model and territories (Cerqueira 2002).
  - ASE 2015 wall-motion scoring is 1–4; there is no separate aneurysm score.
  - A3C apical segment assignment varies between sources.
- **Mechanical complications:** StatPearls and reviews. The pseudoaneurysm neck-ratio direction was left out because sources were unresolved.

## IVUS and stent sizing
- **Guideline classes:**
  - 2024 ESC CCS: Class I-A for complex PCI (LM, true bifurcations, long lesions).
  - 2021 ACC/AHA/SCAI: 2a.
  - 2025 ACC/AHA ACS: Class 1, from secondary sources.
  - In 2026, IVUS-CHIP and OPTIMAL were neutral.
- **MLA thresholds:** LM <6 mm² Western (LITRO), 4.5–4.8 mm² Asian. Non-LM 4 mm² is a poor FFR predictor.
- **Optimization:**
  - EAPCI 2018: MSA >5.5 mm² IVUS, >80% of reference.
  - ULTIMATE: MSA >5.0 mm² or ≥90% of distal reference lumen, edge plaque burden <50%, no medial dissection >3 mm.
  - IVUS-XPL: MSA > distal reference lumen area.
  - LM: Kang 5-6-7-8; EXCEL ~9.8 mm².
- **Malapposition:** correct if >0.4 mm and >1 mm long (OCT-derived).
- **Edge dissection:** treat if medial and ≥60° or ≥3 mm. Sources differ slightly on the length cut-off.
- **Sizing:** EEM-based (round down) vs lumen-based; MLD MAX (Shlofmitz 2022).
- **Angiographic sizing:** stent:artery 1.0–1.1 and the guide-catheter ruler are **[standard teaching]**.
- **IVUS images:** generated by ray-marching a tissue model (echogenicity, attenuation, speckle). Teaching approximations.

## Bifurcation and ostial
- **Finet's law:** D = 0.678 × (D1 + D2). Murray and Huo–Kassab are also shown.
- **DEFINITION criteria:** Chen et al.
- **Trials:** EBC MAIN, DKCRUSH-V, DEFINITION II, Nordic I/III/IV.
- **Technique sequences** follow EBC consensus descriptions **[standard teaching; step wording not re-verified against full text]**.
- **"JL crush":** no technique by that name was found. It is presumed to mean DK crush.
- **Ostial techniques:** 1–2 mm aortic protrusion, flaring, Szabo, crossover **[standard teaching]**.

## CICU cases
Two more research passes (November 2026) checked the following.
- **Shock definitions:** SHOCK trial criteria (SBP <90 for >30 min or vasopressors, hypoperfusion, CI ≤2.2 with PCWP ≥15) and IABP-SHOCK II criteria. SCAI SHOCK stages (2019, 2022 update).
- **CPO:** Fincke 2004; ≤0.53 W carried about 58% mortality, and many use <0.6 W.
- **PAPi:** ≤0.9 after proximal RCA infarct (Korabathina 2012, n=20); <1.85 after LVAD (Morine 2016).
- **Trials:**
  - SHOCK: 30-day mortality 46.7% vs 56.0%, NS; 6-month 50.3% vs 63.1%.
  - CULPRIT-SHOCK: 30-day death or RRT 45.9% vs 55.4%; the 1-year mortality difference was NS.
  - IABP-SHOCK II: 39.7% vs 41.3%.
  - ECLS-SHOCK: 47.8% vs 49.0%; bleeding 23.4% vs 9.6%.
  - DanGer Shock: 45.8% vs 58.5%; safety composite 24.0% vs 6.2%; RRT 41.9% vs 26.7%.
  - SOAP II: arrhythmias 24.1% vs 12.4%. The cardiogenic shock subgroup result is exploratory (interaction P=0.87).
  - DOREMI: no difference.
  - COMMIT: cardiogenic shock 5.0% vs 3.9%.
- **2025 ACC/AHA ACS guideline:**
  - Culprit revascularization: Class 1.
  - Routine non-culprit PCI at the index procedure in shock: 3: Harm.
  - Microaxial pump in selected STEMI shock: 2a.
  - Routine IABP and routine VA-ECMO: 3: No Benefit. These two come from secondary summaries; the full text was not readable here.
- **Post-MI care:**
  - EPHESUS; EMPACT-MI (primary endpoint NS, fewer HF hospitalizations); DAPA-MI.
  - VEST: primary endpoint negative.
  - ICD timing (≥40 days after MI, ≥90 days after revascularization).
  - 2022 AHA LV thrombus statement: about 3 months of anticoagulation; DOAC reasonable.
- **Mechanical complications:**
  - Damluji 2021 AHA statement, read via its summary.
  - Papillary muscle rupture: emergency MV replacement is the treatment of choice; TEER for high surgical risk.
  - VSR timing: STS data show 54% vs 18% operative mortality for repair ≤7 vs >7 days, with survivor bias. 87% of European centres prefer delayed repair bridged on MCS.
  - Free-wall rupture: up to a third are subacute; CAUTION cohort operative mortality 36%.
- **AS:**
  - 2020 ACC/AHA severe AS criteria and stage D2 (dobutamine stress echo "reasonable").
  - Khot 2003: CI 1.60 → 2.22 at 6 h → 2.52 at 24 h; MAP titrated to 60–70; patients on pressors or with MAP <60 were excluded. The case was written to match.
  - BAV as a bridge: 2b.
  - Vasopressor choice in AS is expert opinion only.
- **VT storm:** 2017 AHA/ACC/HRS and 2022 ESC (ablation for drug-refractory storm); VANISH 59.1% vs 68.5%; PROCAMIO (tolerated wide-QRS tachycardia, not storm); propranolol vs metoprolol (Chatzidou 2018).
- **Type A dissection:** 2022 ACC/AHA targets are SBP <120 and **HR 60–80**; beta-blocker before vasodilator; immediate surgery Class 1.
- **PE:**
  - ESC 2019: systemic lysis Class I for high-risk PE; alteplase 100 mg over 2 h (an accelerated 0.6 mg/kg regimen also exists).
  - New 2026 AHA/ACC PE guideline with clinical categories A–E. Secondary sources conflict on the class of advanced therapies in category E.
- **Not verified:** exact fluid volumes in RV infarction, the size of the 30–45% pseudoaneurysm rupture risk, and the exact 2025 guideline wording for the microaxial pump.
